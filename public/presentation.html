FORMULA: ADC_Sample = (1/8) * Σ analogReadMilliVolts(PIN_ADC)

To eliminate ghosting and cross-talk across 100 intersections, the ESP32 drives one row HIGH at a time while setting unselected rows to high-impedance (Hi-Z). A CD74HC4067 multiplexer samples each column with 8x hardware oversampling to eliminate 50/60Hz AC noise.
